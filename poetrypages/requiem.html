—— The Requiem (Arrow) ——

Source: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Wind

The mysterious arrow that grants a "Requiem" to those who are worthy and have endured struggles and sacrifice.

In JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure, a Requiem represents the absolute, ultimate evolution of a Stand (ability), triggered when the Stand itself, rather than its user, is pierced by the Stand Arrow.

Much like a musical requiem (a mass for the dead), obtaining a state of Requiem requires great conviction and sacrifice, culminating the end of a long struggle. The user of an ability has to pierce their own ability, sacrificing it, in order to evolve and rebirth to its full potential.

It symbolizes that death, loss, and struggle are not the end. They can be catalysts, an opportunity for something new and better to arise. One must have the resolve to endure and to sacrifice in order for them to grow and be better, much like dying in order to be reborn.
