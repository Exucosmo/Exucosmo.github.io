—— All-Imperfect Love Song ——

Source: ULTRAKILL (Videogame)

ALL-IMPERFECT LOVE SONG is the title of a secret level in Ultrakill. It starts off comedic and absurdist, putting the player in a "dating simulator" visual novel aesthetic with the main character robot being dressed up as an anime girl, but it quickly catches you by surprise with a tonal shift, devolving into a serious discussion about existence and existencial dread.

I've experienced existential dread since the pandemic when I was forced to be alone for a long time with my own thoughts, but quickly distracted myself with F2F school and never really resolved those thoughts about the future. This secret level not only captures that dreadful feeling and spiraling train of thought perfectly but also gave me a good message and closure.

IN-GAME DIALOGUE HIGHLIGHT:


"By continually forcing to subvert those creeping negative thoughts with the positive logical ones, the emotional mind will eventually, slowly, gradually start to shape to fit the logical. It is not an easy job. It is not a quick job. It will sometimes even feel like an impossible job. However, it can be done. With an immense amount of effort and energy, it will improve. You can change. You can heal. And during the hardest times, when all seems lost and you want to give up, never forget: we will always love you."
