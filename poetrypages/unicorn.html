—— Unicorn Gundam ——

Source: Mobile Suit Gundam Unicorn


Associated Words: Beast Of Possibility

The Unicorn Gundam symbolizes hope, human potential, and the beast of possibility.

Throughout the entire series, it manages to achieve impossible miraculous feats that the Gundam universe has never seen before. It and it's pilot Banagher embodies the unpredictable yet vital nature of human potential and the belief that the future can change for the better. Banagher believes that "possibility" is an innate human power capable of transcending fate and breaking through a cold, bleak future.

The possibility of a better future is worth living and fighting for.
