—— Aerial Gundam ——

Source: Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury

The Aerial Gundam represents technology that is used to protect humanity rather than destroy it, leading to a peaceful future.

Traditional Gundams in the series were universally banned because of their ability to harm and kill their pilots with mental strain when using them. Mobile suits are also machines inherently built for destruction and war. But Aerial breaks that curse, able to harmoniously work with its pilot without harming them.

Aerial is the herald of a future that is hopeful and benevolent, where youth break the cycle of trauma. The word Aerial itself evokes a sense of flight and freedom.
