—— Permet Score 8 ——

Source: Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury

Associated Words: Light Of Hope, Rejection Of Conflict

When Suletta reaches Permet Score 8 with Gundam Calibarn, an unprecedented feat of power and synchronization, she and Eri (Aerial) transcends into a rainbow-like luminescence and complete harmony of consciousness. She links multiple mobile suits together in order to explode into a HUGE, bright data storm that lit up space.

Calibarn Permet Score 8 symbolizes the complete rejection of using technology for destruction, disintegrating all the mobile suits which are built for conflict into dust and deactivating the mass destruction colony laser. It represents the power of mutual understanding, human connections, and empathy as a light to triumph over war.
