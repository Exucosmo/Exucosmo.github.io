—— Roundabout Path (Zeppeli's Final Lesson) ——

Source: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Steel Ball Run

Associated Ideas: The Indirect and Winding Path

Gyro Zeppeli, the mentor figure of the main character Johnny Joestar, gives his final lesson before his death at the final fight.

Steel Ball Run is a massive horse race across the entire North American continent, and thus racers need to get to their checkpoints as fast as possible. But Gyro remarks that the detours they experienced were actually what they really needed all along. This quote captures the paradox that looking for direct shortcuts yield hollow results but enduring the obstacles along the roundabout path forges your strengths and character in order to truly achieve your goal.

A detour is never a wasted path, it's where true wisdom and character are cultivated. The various obstacles and difficulties they faced, and the times they've had to slow down to help people along the roundabout, ultimately allowed them to grow as people and defeat the main villain. So, ironically, looking for shortcuts is not the most effective way to achieve your goal, but enduring the struggle. The roundabout path.
