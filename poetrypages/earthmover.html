—— 1000-THR "Earthmover" ——

Source: ULTRAKILL (Videogame)

The 1000-THR "Earthmover" is the apex design and culmination of the Final War of humanity in the ULTRAKILL universe, a massive behemoth of a mass-destruction machine that towers over and wipes out entire cities with ease. They represent mankind's worst qualities and light up the night in a different way: by the sheer power of their weapons and the destruction they leave in their wake.

It is the game's stand-in for the mythological Centaur, and is the absolute biggest machine humanity has created in the mechanical arms race, perpetuating a cycle of violence and war towards other humans that has lasted for over 200 years. They represent mankind's tendency for cruelty and conflict and a reminder of man's greatest obstacle: mankind's own nature.
